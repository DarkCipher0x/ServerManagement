# ServerManagement 0.5.7 (2026-10-21) – DarkCipher Systems

The app shows your profile's name instead of your PC and Windows account.

## Fixed
- **"Approved by PC\user"** in Settings → Security & privacy → Admin protection named the PC and the Windows
  account. Admin approvals now record and show your **profile's name** (set it in Settings → Profiles → Rename, or in
  Quick Start). Approvals recorded before 0.5.7 show the new name too. A change approved by a *different* Windows
  administrator is still recorded under that administrator's account, because who approved it is the fact.
- The same goes for the other places your account or PC name appeared:
  - the admin-approval windows, which now lead with your name and keep the account beside it so an administrator can
    still see exactly who is asking;
  - the label in your authenticator app for new 2FA set-ups, and the recovery codes text;
  - the default name for new SSH keys, and your name in shared libraries;
  - the Windows sign-in prompt, which no longer spells out the account.

## Security
- A profile name is chosen by its user, so it is display only: approvals are still checked against the Windows account,
  and the approval windows still show that account. Log sinks and syslog keep the Windows account and PC, which an
  organisation's records need. Names are cleaned of control characters and capped at 64 characters.
