# DarkCipher ServerManagement 0.1.16 (2026-10-19)

## New features

### Shared library: finished
- **Hand over ownership.** First make the new owner an admin. Then, as the owner, use Shared library → **Hand over ownership…** and pick them. Nothing changes until they press **Accept ownership…** on their PC. You stay an admin, and every member stays valid. Team add-ons made before or after the hand-over keep working.
- **Back up and restore your keys.** **⚿ Back up my keys…** saves a `.dcxkey` file sealed with your password (at least 12 characters; PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations, then AES-256-GCM). To use it on a new PC or after a reinstall, install the library's add-on and choose **⇩ Restore my keys…**. The owner should always keep one: the owner key exists only on their PC.
- **Add-ons screen.** Library add-ons are now described as libraries, not themes. Their SHARED LIBRARY section shows whether this PC has joined, your role and the last sync, with a button that opens the Shared library screen.

## Fixes
- **Shared library:** demoting an admin to member made the keys of collections they had last changed unreadable for everyone (the files were still signed by someone who was no longer an admin). The owner now re-signs those files, and the grants that admin made, as part of the change. When someone keeps read access but loses edit rights, the revisions they wrote are signed again by the admin making the change, so they don't disappear.

## Known limitations
- Shared library: anyone with write access to the share can delete or roll back files. This is detected and flagged, not prevented. Reads are audited by each member's own app. It has been tested with simulated members on one PC, not yet on a real share with several PCs.
