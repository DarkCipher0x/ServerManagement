# DarkCipher ServerManagement 0.2.1 (2026-10-20)

The DarkCipher house theme: the look of DarkCipher AgentControl, now shared by every DarkCipher Systems app, with
Settings rebuilt around it. Right-click menus are organised into groups. It upgrades 0.2.0 and every earlier release;
your settings carry over.

## New

### The house theme
- **Colours:** teal on near-black with a deeper card colour, brighter headings and stronger lines. Five presets:
  DarkCipher, Graphite, Teal, High Contrast and Light. An accent from the swatches or your own colour (it must stand out
  at least 3:1 against the background; the check shows the ratio). Status colours subtle, standard or strong. Windows high
  contrast is followed unless you turn that off.
- **Shape and type:** Segoe UI Variable and Cascadia Mono, corners sharp / 2 px / rounded, thinner scrollbars.
  Animation follows Windows, or choose standard or reduced (reduced stops the header cursor blinking).
- **Icons:** the sidebar uses Segoe Fluent Icons.

### Settings
- **Tabs across the top:** Security & privacy, Cloud providers, AI & models, Terminals & connections, Appearance,
  Navigation, Data & backups, Integrations, Import & export, About. The search box stays at the top and searches every
  tab; a match opens its tab.
- **One column of cards**, each with a title, what it applies to, and a **Reset** for that card only. On/off settings are
  switches, drop-downs are the same width, and each setting has a short caption under it.
- **Appearance:** preset, accent, contrast check, status colours, density, text size, zoom, corners, animation and fonts.
- **Navigation:** sidebar style (expanded, compact, or **icons only** with tooltips), sidebar width, the page the app
  opens on, up to six **pinned pages** at the top of the sidebar (or right-click a sidebar item → Pin to the top of the
  sidebar), and the sidebar's sections.
- **Import & export:** save your appearance and navigation settings to a file and load them on another PC. Nothing
  else is in the file (no hosts, credentials or keys); a file is checked and its changes listed before anything applies.
  **Reset all appearance and navigation** puts them back to the defaults.
- **About:** the version, the fonts and icons in use, and how to check a download.

### Right-click menus in groups
- The everyday actions for what you clicked are at the top (Copy / Paste / Find in a terminal; Connect / Files /
  Manage on a host; Close / Rename on a tab). The rest sit under named groups: in the terminal **Selection & notes**,
  **Output & input**, **Capture & share**, **Session**, **Host**, **View**; on a host **Notes & incidents**, **Organise**,
  **Copy & export**, **Remote Desktop**; on a tab **Close tabs**, **Tab group**, **Layout**, **Session**,
  **Screenshot & help**.
- A short menu shows its groups inline between separators; a menu that would run past 12 rows folds each group into a
  submenu. No top-level menu is now longer than 12 rows.
- Lists put the row's own actions first, then the screen's buttons under **Screen actions** and **Copy & export**.

## Theme add-ons
Theme add-ons keep working unchanged (the colour names are the same). Colours the new look adds are worked out from the
add-on's own colours.

## Under the hood
- `Icons.For(glyph)` maps the app's existing glyphs to Fluent icons, so modules and add-ons need no change.
- `Menus.Group(name, items)` declares a group; `Menus.Fill` decides inline or submenu.
- Settings restyles every card it shows (switches, drop-down width, captions), including cards from modules and
  add-ons.
- New self-test section for the house theme: presets and contrast, the Settings layout, import / export, sidebar
  styles, and every menu the app builds. A new guard fails the run if the self-test ever opens a popup menu or tooltip
  on the screen.
- Decision record: ADR-038.

## Known gaps
- WhisperDesk, MaskedMail and the installers don't have the tabbed Settings, switches and Fluent icons yet.
- Updates stay off until the releases are code-signed.
