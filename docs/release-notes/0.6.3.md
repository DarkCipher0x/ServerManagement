# ServerManagement 0.6.3 (2026-10-22) – DarkCipher Systems

Rearrange and pin the pages in the sidebar; the Hosts folder pane's collapsed tab is a compact, crisp "Hosts".

## Added
- **Rearrange the sidebar's pages.** Drag a page up or down within its section, or right-click it → **Move up** /
  **Move down**. Pages stay in their own section; sections still move by dragging their headers. Right-click a section
  header → **Reset sidebar layout** puts the default order back. The order is saved, and is a Group Policy
  (`NavItemOrder`) like the section order.
- **Pin pages more easily.** Point at a page and click its 📌 to pin it to the PINNED section at the top (a pinned page
  shows its pin in the accent colour; click it to unpin). You can also drag a page onto PINNED, or onto a pinned page
  to put it there (with nothing pinned yet, a PINNED drop zone appears while you drag). Drag pinned pages to reorder
  them, or drag one out of PINNED to unpin it; right-click a pinned page → Move up / Move down.

## Changed
- **The Hosts folder pane's collapsed tab** (auto-collapse) is now a slim, flush "Hosts" tab with the hosts icon,
  instead of a "▸ FOLDERS" button: muted, with an edge line that lights up in the accent colour when you point at it.
- **The sliding folder pane is crisp.** Its drop shadow is gone (a shadow effect made WPF draw the pane as a blurred
  bitmap); it now has a solid surface and an edge line.

## Security
- No change to storage, encryption or network access. Page order and pins live in the encrypted settings, as before.
