# ServerManagement 0.6.43 (2026-11-09) – DarkCipher Systems

**Public beta.** Built by an IT engineer, for engineers. Free and open source (MIT). Post-quantum ready.

## Beta: please read
- **This is a beta.** Expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything important (Settings → Data & backups).
- **The installer and the app aren't code-signed yet.** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose
  **More info → Run anyway** only if the SHA-256 of your download matches the one published with the release. The
  update information inside the app *is* signed, twice (ECDSA P-256 and post-quantum ML-DSA-65), and the app refuses an
  update unless both signatures match.
- **Testers are very welcome**, especially for CipherChat calls and live session sharing against your own CipherChat
  server, and Windows management against remote computers.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** please use the forms on darkciphersystems.com (Report a bug, and Feature requests →
  ServerManagement).

## New
- **Add-ons can have their own sidebar section.** A module add-on (signed with the release keys) can add a section of
  its own, for example CipherMail's **EMAIL**, placed after the section it names. You move, hide, expand and pin it like
  any other section, and its place is kept across restarts, also while the add-on is turned off. Before this, items for
  a section the app didn't know were dropped without a word.
- **Live sidebar entries.** An add-on can list entries that change while the app runs, such as one entry per mail
  account with its unread count after the name (99+ above 99). They open, pin and hide like any page, and the sidebar
  refreshes when the add-on says its list changed.
- **Add-on icons.** An add-on can draw its own glyphs as Segoe Fluent icons, like the app's own pages (it can't change
  the app's icons). The app also knows CipherMail's icons for Mail, Accounts, Contacts and Masked Email.
- **Add-ons can name the oldest app they need.** `addon.json` may carry `"minAppVersion"`: an older app refuses the
  install with "Update the app first", and doesn't load a module add-on that needs a newer app (for example after a
  rollback). Apps before 0.6.43 ignore it.

## For add-on authors
- New, additive points on `IAppHost` (module API stays 1): `NavSection(key, glyph, after)`, `Icon(glyph, codePoint)`,
  `NavItems(section, items)` with `ModuleNavEntry`, and `RefreshNav()`. See docs/ADDONS.md section 14 and ADR-121 /
  ADR-121a in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md. On older apps these are missing: catch `MissingMethodException`.

## Fixed
- The self-test's module check now accepts any installed module add-on next to the built-in ones, instead of failing
  when one other than the stream manager is installed.
