# DarkCipher ServerManagement 0.2.0-preview.1 (2026-10-20)

A preview of 0.2.0: the app can now run with **no AI at all**, and that choice can be locked with a password.
The installer version is 0.1.21, so the final 0.2.0 will upgrade it.

## New

### AI features: on or off
- **Settings → Security & privacy → AI features** decides whether the app has AI at all. Off means no AI screens, sidebar items, "✦ … with AI" menu items, AI settings, AI help articles, background AI work, model downloads or engine connections. Nothing is hidden: the AI part is simply not loaded.
- Offline features keep working with AI off: log digests, rule-based health and fleet reports, help search.
- Your AI settings and chats stay in the encrypted store, so turning AI back on restores them.
- A change applies after a restart; the card has a **Restart now** button.
- **New installs start with AI off.** Upgrading from 0.1.x keeps AI on if you were using it (enabled, with an engine).

### The AI features lock
- **Lock with a password…** (at least 12 characters) on the same card. The password is then needed to turn AI features on or off, to allow internet AI providers, and to change or remove the lock.
- After three wrong passwords the next try waits 30 seconds, doubling up to 10 minutes. Restarting the app doesn't reset the wait.
- Every attempt is in the audit log, never what was typed. Only a salted verifier is stored, encrypted.
- There is no recovery for a forgotten password, by design. Deleting `ai-lock.dcx` from the data folder (app closed) removes the lock, and the audit log records that at the next start.

### For administrators
- Machine policy `HKLM\SOFTWARE\Policies\DarkCipher Systems\ServerManagement`, DWORD `AiFeatures`: 0 forces AI off, 1 forces it on, for every user. The card then says it is set by your administrator.
- The MSI writes the 0: `msiexec /i DarkCipherServerManagement-0.2.0-preview.1-x64.msi AIFEATURES=0` (pass it on every upgrade, or use Group Policy).
- `DCX_AI_FEATURES=0` or `1` overrides the setting for one run, except while the lock is set.

### Add-ons
- With AI off, the six capabilities that act on the AI Engineer's target terminal (`terminal_run`, `terminal_read_screen`, `terminal_send_keys`, `terminal_interrupt`, `terminal_switch_target`, `terminal_open_session`) are not listed; calling one returns "capability unavailable (AI off)". Everything else is unchanged.

### Privacy & network
- A new **AI features** row says whether the AI module is loaded, and whether it is locked or set by policy.

## Changed
- With AI off, the sidebar's AI section is called **REPORTS & HELP**; the server's AI insights tab is called **Insights**.
- A Vultr catalogue entry (Serverless Inference) no longer uses the ✦ mark, which the app keeps for its own AI actions.

## Under the hood (the 0.2.0 restructure, `docs/history/PLAN-0.2.0.md`, phases 3 to 5)
- Everything AI is now one module (`Views\AiModule.cs`) that adds itself to the screens through extension points: the sidebar, Settings, menus, the error box, the right dock, shortcuts, the dashboard, the privacy report, help articles, an assistant service for "✦ … with AI" actions, and the report analysis.
- Reports, log digests and the help search moved out of the AI code; they work without it.
- The self-test now runs twice: the full suite with AI on, and a second run with AI off that opens every sidebar screen, a server view and a terminal and checks that no AI text, item or process exists anywhere.

## Preview notes
- Everything with AI on behaves as in 0.1.20.
- Not in this preview: the split into separate projects (phase 6), the extra architecture guards (phase 7) and the final documentation pass (phase 8).
