# ServerManagement 0.5.8 (2026-10-21) – DarkCipher Systems

Hosts can stay open beside your sessions, pinned or auto-collapsing.

## Added
- **Hosts beside the sessions.** Choose it under Hosts → More ▾ → When you connect…, or in Settings → Navigation →
  Sidebar → Hosts when you connect:
  - **Pinned:** Hosts sits in a panel on the left of your sessions and stays open whatever you connect to. Drag its
    edge to resize it; the width is remembered.
  - **Auto-collapse:** the same panel folds to a slim "Hosts" strip as soon as you connect or click into a session.
    Click the strip, or choose Hosts in the sidebar, to open it again.
  - **Tab** (the default, as before): Hosts is an ordinary tab and sessions open in front of it.

  The panel's 📌 / 📍 button switches between pinned and auto-collapse, and ⧉ Tab puts Hosts back as a tab. It is the
  same Hosts screen throughout, so your folder, filter and selection carry over. Administrators can set it by Group
  Policy (`HostsDock`: tab, pinned or auto).

## Security
- No change to storage, encryption, network access or protected settings. One new Group Policy (`HostsDock`), now 215
  in all.
