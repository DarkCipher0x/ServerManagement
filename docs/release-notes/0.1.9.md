# DarkCipher ServerManagement 0.1.9 (2026-10-18)

## The product is now "DarkCipher ServerManagement"
The new name is used everywhere the app or the installer shows it:
- the window title and the header at the top of the main window;
- pop-out windows, document windows and the AI Engineer window;
- the installer: its title, the licence page, the finish page ("Launch DarkCipher ServerManagement") and its messages;
- the Start menu and desktop shortcuts, the *Repair or uninstall* shortcut, and the *Installed apps* entry;
- the file properties of `DcxServerManager.exe` (product name and description);
- reports, exported snippets, document footers, 2FA recovery codes and the 2FA account label for new set-ups;
- the built-in help, the AI prompts, and this guide.

**What doesn't change**, so an upgrade keeps everything:
- the program folder (`C:\Program Files\DarkCipher Systems\ServerManagement`);
- the data folders (`%APPDATA%` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DarkCipher\ServerManagement`);
- the registry key, the upgrade identity and the executable name (`DcxServerManager.exe`).

**Two side effects:**
- New screenshots go to `Pictures\DarkCipher ServerManagement`; earlier ones stay in the old folder.
- An authenticator app that already has this app's 2FA keeps the old label. Codes keep working.

## Also in this version
- **The PDF guide is back**, with chapters for 0.1.7, 0.1.8 and this release, and the architecture decisions up to ADR-027 (this rename).
- **The administration chapter** describes the automatic Foundry Local set-up from 0.1.8 and this rename.

## Before you rely on it
- **Verified by the automated self-test** (no failures; see TESTING.md for the count).
- **Not code-signed:** SmartScreen shows "unknown publisher", and in-app updates stay switched off.
