# ServerManagement 0.6.41 (2026-11-09) – DarkCipher Systems

**Public beta.** Built by an IT engineer, for engineers. Free and open source (MIT). Post-quantum ready.

## Beta: please read
- **This is a beta.** Expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything important (Settings → Data & backups).
- **The installer and the app aren't code-signed yet.** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose
  **More info → Run anyway** only if the SHA-256 of your download matches the one published with the release. The
  update information inside the app *is* signed, twice (ECDSA P-256 and post-quantum ML-DSA-65), and the app refuses an
  update unless both signatures match.
- **Testers are very welcome**, especially for CipherChat calls and live session sharing against your own CipherChat
  server, and Windows management against remote computers.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** please use the forms on darkciphersystems.com (Report a bug, and Feature requests →
  ServerManagement).

## New
- **Chats look and work like CipherChat.** The Chats screen is now laid out as CipherChat's own Windows client: on the
  left your accounts, then the selected account's **ROOMS**, **DIRECT** messages (each with **+** to start one) and
  **PEOPLE** with their trust (verified, pending, no keys, untrusted; click a person for the safety number, and the owner
  certifies or revokes there). The room shows in place on the right: its header with the topic and the room key
  (`key #2 · 2/2 hold it · 21h old`), Voice, Video, Members, Room settings, Pin and Open as tab; the terminal-style log
  (time, name, message) in the monospace font; and the prompt `you@cipherchat:#room$` with emoji, GIF, burn after
  reading (click to step 10 s → 30 s → 1 min → 5 min), attach and Send, over a status line with the post-quantum suites,
  your identity and the auto-lock. Room tabs and the side panel use the same view (icon-only in narrow places).
- **Everything CipherChat does in a room.** Right-click a message to react (quick reactions or any emoji), reply, edit
  your own, copy, or delete for everyone; emoji show in colour as you type and in messages; GIFs play inline and the
  GIF button sends one again from this session (in memory only); paste or drop files and screenshots; Esc cancels a
  reply, an edit or the files. Messages still on their way show queued / sending / not sent with **retry** and
  **discard**. Slash commands: `/help`, `/join`, `/new`, `/me`, `/topic`, `/invite`, `/kick`, `/members`,
  `/call`, `/video`, `/hangup`, `/rotate`, `/verify`, `/fp`, `/leave`, `/clear`, `/lock` (`//` sends a line that
  starts with /). `/help` lists them in two columns, each command on its own line. New rooms (name, topic, history, members), members (add, remove, new key) and room settings (name,
  topic, how long messages and keys are kept, history sync, delete) are CipherChat's own dialogs.

## Fixed
- **The chat lists flashed white** while unlocking or signing in (the lists were disabled for the moment and Windows'
  default disabled look showed through). Lists, list views and trees now always draw in the theme.
- **Notices from the chat engine weren't shown** (a contact's key changed, a key renewal, server notices): they now
  appear in the room's log, as in CipherChat.
- **Another of your devices asking for history got no answer.** ServerManagement now asks you, with the device's code
  to compare (Approve, Decline or Not now), as CipherChat does; your answer is audited.
- The live run's share check measured the other person's own camera preview instead of the shared window; it now
  reads only the received share, with the shared window made square so its shape can't be confused with a screen.
