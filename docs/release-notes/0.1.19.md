# DarkCipher ServerManagement 0.1.19 (2026-10-19)

## Fixes

### First-launch layout
- **The AI Engineer sidebar no longer squeezes the open screen.**
  - The docked panel now takes at most 40 % of the window, and the screen beside it always keeps at least 620 px.
  - A wider saved width comes back on a bigger window, and the panel shrinks and grows as you resize the window.
- **Dashboard heading:**
  - "Engineering overview" is never cut off; Check now, Checks and Customise move under the title when the screen is narrow.
  - The date and "Remote checks…" lines use the full width instead of a narrow column.
