# DarkCipher ServerManagement 0.2.2 (2026-10-20)

A follow-up to 0.2.1: softer text in dark mode, and the one part of the new Navigation tab that 0.2.1 missed. It
upgrades 0.2.1 and every earlier release.

## Changed
- **Softer text in dark mode.** Body text and headings were close to pure white on a near-black background, which
  glared. They are now dialled back about 10 %: DarkCipher text #BCD8D5 (was #D2F2EF), headings #D6EEEB (was #EAFBF9);
  Graphite and Teal likewise, and the header follows. Text is still about 12–13:1 against the background, well
  above the 7:1 the house theme requires.
- **High Contrast keeps pure white**, and light mode is unchanged.
- Exported HTML reports and the OBS broadcast page use the same softer text colour.

## New
- **Shown pages** (Settings → Navigation → Navigation & explorers): a switch for every sidebar page, or right-click a
  page in the sidebar → Hide. A hidden page leaves the sidebar (and its pin) but still opens from links, menus and quick
  connect. Settings is always shown. Hidden pages are part of the appearance export and of that card's Reset.

## Fixes
- The help no longer points at two internal setting names (the reconnect cap and reopening tabs after a crash).

## Under the hood
- The self-test now also builds the terminal's right-click menu (recorded, never opened) and checks it stays within
  12 top-level rows, and checks that a theme add-on gets the house theme's extra colours from its own palette.
- A right-click inside a terminal during the self-test no longer leaves WebView2 waiting for a menu that never opens.

## Theme add-ons
Unchanged: an add-on that sets its own text colour keeps it.
