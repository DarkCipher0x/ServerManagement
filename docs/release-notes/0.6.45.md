# ServerManagement 0.6.45 (2026-11-09) – DarkCipher Systems

**Public beta.** Built by an IT engineer, for engineers. Free and open source (MIT). Post-quantum ready.

## Beta: please read
- **This is a beta.** Expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything important (Settings → Data & backups).
- **The installer and the app aren't code-signed yet.** Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose
  **More info → Run anyway** only if the SHA-256 of your download matches the one published with the release. The
  update information inside the app *is* signed, twice (ECDSA P-256 and post-quantum ML-DSA-65), and the app refuses an
  update unless both signatures match.
- **Testers are very welcome**, especially for CipherChat calls and live session sharing against your own CipherChat
  server, and Windows management against remote computers.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** please use the forms on darkciphersystems.com (Report a bug, and Feature requests →
  ServerManagement).

## New
## New
- **Close the hosts panel, bring it back when you need it.** The panel now has three modes: pinned open beside every
  tab (default), auto-collapse beside every tab, or **only on the Hosts tab**. Click **✕** on the panel's header (or
  right-click the Hosts strip → Close the hosts panel) and nothing sits beside your other tabs any more; the panel stays
  on the Hosts tab. Open Hosts from the sidebar and click **📌** on its panel to pin it beside every tab again. The same
  three choices are in Settings → Appearance → Hosts panel, and Group Policy `HostsDock` now accepts `hosts`.

## Fixed
- **Sidebar counts no longer rebuild the sidebar.** When an add-on's live entries change only in their counts (unread
  mail, for example) the numbers are updated in place. Rebuilding the whole sidebar each time could leave a session's
  embedded view, such as an open email, drawn black until the window was laid out again.
